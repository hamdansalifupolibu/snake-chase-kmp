## From the original Snake Chase

Supplied by the author from the first version of the game: the 3-2-1-GO countdown beep
(`countdown_beep.mp3`), *Eclipse* by Alegend (`bgm_eclipse.mp3`), the near-miss whoosh,
the game-over impact and the snake hiss. Their original sources are being confirmed.

The optional **meme roasts** (`meme_*.mp3`, Settings → Meme roasts) are short internet meme clips
from the original game. Their copyright is not confirmed; they are planned to be replaced with
original or CC0 recordings before a store release.

## Code and tools

Kotlin, Compose Multiplatform, Ktor, Koin, kotlinx libraries, multiplatform-settings, Firebase;
on desktop, MP3 decoding uses JLayer/mp3spi (LGPL). The Kenney clips were converted from OGG to WAV so every platform plays them natively. The app icon is
generated by `tools/generate_icon.py` from the game's own painting.
